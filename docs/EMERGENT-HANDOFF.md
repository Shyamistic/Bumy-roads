# Human-only Emergent, Video, and Submission Handoff

The project can prepare a local portfolio interface. Shyam alone must decide what to say, record the voice, choose public contact details, approve consent and redaction, publish, record a video, and submit the result. No credentials are requested, stored, or used by this repository.

1. Review every claim, public link, reflection, and proposed-result label. Correct or remove anything that is not accurate.
2. Add only authentic Shyam recordings that have affirmative release approval. Verify captions and transcripts against the final files.
3. Confirm any photo, screenshot, voice, quote, screen, or identifiable detail is consent-cleared and redacted where needed.
4. Decide whether any additional public contact method is wanted. A phone number remains absent unless Shyam explicitly opts in.
5. Build and preview locally. Verify the empty and ready Voice Notes states, keyboard controls, error feedback, no autoplay, no microphone request, reduced motion, forced colors, no-JavaScript content, and all source links.
6. Manually use Shyam’s own Emergent account to publish a public link, if he chooses to use Emergent. This code does not log in to, publish through, or claim an integration with Emergent.
7. Record the required video in Shyam’s own voice explaining what was built, why, and what voice contributed. Do not present generated narration as personal testimony.
8. Manually complete the challenge submission with the public portfolio URL, video, and grant response. Recheck the destination and challenge requirements immediately before submission.

MIT attribution to the ITomPoland starter must remain. Original ITom personal assets and copy are not used. This portfolio does not imply endorsement by ITom, Wispr Flow, or Emergent.
